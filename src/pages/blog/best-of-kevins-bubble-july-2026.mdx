### [The AI Hype Reckoning is upon us](https://karlbode.com/the-ai-hype-reckoning-is-upon-us/)

I know, the AI hate topics almost feel like old news (at least in my bubble, as I tend to follow feeds that tend towards AI hate). Still, I found this article quite good as it consolidated a bunch of "lay off and rehire" or "lay-offs followed by regret" stories that just underlines one of the main problems I see with the AI topic. Managers, Human beings that think AI is actually intelligent and can make decisions for them, while it is just another chatbot that tells you what you want to hear, while having some other helpful generative tools. Similar to how Social Media algorithms of big tech platforms tend to show you what you like anyways, further pushing you into a corner and forgetting that the real world has way more to offer.

Well, most of the chatbot companies are still only alive due to their investment money. Let's see for how much longer this works out.
