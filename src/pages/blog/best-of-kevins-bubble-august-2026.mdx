## Links

### [Don't paste AI](https://dontpastetheai.com/)

This one really hit home with me. I need to admit that I also hate it when I realize that someone didn't even took the time to write in their own words what they have to say. Since I of course also use a bot for my code reviews and also let the bot comment on GitLab I started adding a "🤖 **KVN** (bot) speaking 🤖" prefix. So it is on the one hand-side easier for my colleagues to know who actually answered but also for me easier to distinguish between what I wrote and what I prompted. Let's see this month how good that prefix works out.

### [LLMs reward expertise](https://www.seangoedecke.com/llms-reward-expertise/)

Another chatbot topic and another one that I can't proof of course, but I do get the same feeling. As a frontend engineer myself seeing the difference between the code that "my" bot generates and the code that the bot of non frontend engineers generate is always quite fascinating. And I guess it does make sense. I always need to think about Optimization lessons we had at University in which we had a 3 dimensional "mountain range" with many valleys and dependent on where our optimization algorithm started, it found a different local minimum. If it was lucky it actually found the global minimum, but usually it doesn't.

Giving a bot the context an "expert" of the field has probably gives it a "better" starting point that leads it closer to something that expert actually wants. This is by the way also the reason why I talk to my bot like as if I was talking to a coworker. Because, since it is trained on human text and since I am convinced that people work better when you talk to them in a nice tone, it feels only logical to me that the bot also gives better results if you are nice to it. But again, nothing I can really proof. At least I know that I also feel better when I don't treat it like a code generating slave, but like a helpful and loveable tool.

### [Floor 796](https://floor796.com/)

Be ready to get lost for some minutes 😄 Next to the nice art style and the easter eggs EVERY WHERE, I also learned that the creators of this came up with their own video format in order to support this website. As a frontend developer myself I also wondered how they did, if it's overlapping gifs, or some big video, but then how does it load so smoothly? Well that is exactly why they came up with their own video format in order to support this lazy loading of the animation that we can see on the site. Stuff like this is what the web was made for 😄
