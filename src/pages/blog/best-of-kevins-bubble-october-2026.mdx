## Books

### [Kill 6 billion demons](https://killsixbilliondemons.com/)

Not really a book but still kind of reading so I'll mention it anyways 😄

Kill 6 billion demons is a web comic from the guy that also did the art for the [Lancer TTRPG](https://massifpress.com/lancer). When a friend of mine (the one that also introduced me to Lancer) told me that the same artist has this web comic that is going on for quite a while I put it on my reading list and then didn't open the page for a few months. This month I finally took the time to start reading it and boy is it awesome. As with many stories I read, I like that the main character is a woman. I like what kind of absurd world the author came up with and the art itself is also just stunning.

Can definitely recommend 👌
